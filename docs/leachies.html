Dimension of Leachies

A reptile hobby studio dedicated to leachianus geckos and lizards

What is Dimension of Leachies?

Dimension of Leachies (巨人维度) is my personal reptile hobby studio focused on keeping, studying, and sharing knowledge about Rhacodactylus leachianus — the leachianus gecko, or “leachie” — the largest gecko species in the world. The studio also works with a variety of other lizard species.

The name reflects both the impressive size of these animals (“giant” in Chinese: 巨人) and the layered, complex world of reptile keeping.

The animals

Rhacodactylus leachianus — Leachianus gecko

Leachianus geckos are native to New Caledonia, a group of islands in the South Pacific. They are nocturnal, arboreal animals that can live for 20+ years in captivity. They’re known for their:

  • Impressive size — adults reach 25–35 cm and can weigh over 150g
  • Vocalizations — they produce growls, barks, and hisses unlike any other gecko
  • Personality — each animal has a distinct temperament, from docile to feisty
  • Stunning morphs — a wide range of natural color and pattern variations

Other species

In addition to leachies, the studio works with several other lizard species, including:

  • Crested geckos (Correlophus ciliatus)
  • Gargoyle geckos (Rhacodactylus auriculatus)
  • Other New Caledonian species

Husbandry philosophy

At Dimension of Leachies, I prioritize animal welfare above all else. This means:

  • Providing enclosures that mimic natural habitat in size, structure, and climate
  • Feeding high-quality, varied diets
  • Minimizing unnecessary handling stress
  • Sourcing animals from reputable, ethical breeders

I believe that good husbandry is rooted in understanding the ecology of the animals in their native environment.
